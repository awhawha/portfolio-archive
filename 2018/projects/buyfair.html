Buy Fair: Find your favorite handmade craft made by fair trade material

About the project

Buy fair is a mobile website that selling fair trade crafts, which are designed by artists or designer. It shares the producer's story and the production process.

Timeline

Nov. 2014 to Dec. 2014

Project Detail

Independent project

My Role

wireframing, prototyping, mobile web development(HTML, CSS, jQuery), user testing

VISIT WEBSITE

CONTEXT

I love handmade crafts especially wood and leather. When I have the opportunity to work on my own project, I always want to take the opportunity to help the underprivileged group. In this project, I would like to find a creative way to help people who live in the third world have a better life.

PROCESS

The problem

The solution

The idea behind "Buy Fair" is to invite artists and designers to create their design by using fair trade material. For a buyer, they can know more about how the fair trade material is produced and the story about the people who live in the third world.

Who are the target audiences?

  • 25-40 years old female, who loves handicrafts, has middle to high income and a good taste of lifestyle.
  • Product designer, who focus on wood and leather design.

Usability Testing

Who are my participants?
My participants are people who love art, design, and beautiful things. They love organic food, care about the environmental issue. They love to go to craft fair events, such as RCF and buy the unique products to wear, use or decorate their home.

How many people did I test?
I found seven people at school and coffee shop to help me test the prototype.

Testing Result

After finishing the first round of usability testing, I summed up with a list of problems as below that can be improved in the future.

  • Add the product price
  • Add favorite icon on the product detail page
  • Remove favorite item
  • Add back button to the previous page

    • NEXT STEP
