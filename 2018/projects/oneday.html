One Day: A romantic movie title sequence

About the project

One day is a romantic drama film directed by Long Scherfig and released in 2011. In this project, I decided to create a 1.5 - 2 minutes animation that go through the places that the main actor and actress went before in the movie.

Timeline

Mar. 2014 to May. 2014

Project Detail

Independent project

My Role

research, storyboarding, styleframing, animation.

Movie Title Sequence - One Day from Grace Lee on Vimeo.

CONTEXT

Because this is a romantic film and set the background story in 1988's Edinburgh. I think the watercolor painting style can represent the beauty of the Europe pretty well. Hence, I decided to draw the scenes that have appeared in the movie and used the camara to travel through the scenes.

PROCESS

Storyboard

I started to draw the storyboard first and decided to show the four places in the movie, from Scotland to London. In the storyboard, I also defined the camera path and the order for the ink reveal transition.

The Animation

In order to show the layers in different time, I created different sizes and shapes of ink matte in the After Effects, and then apply it in each layer. Camera movement is the most challenging thing in this project. Because I already created a perspective screen in the Photoshop, it took for a while to adjust the camera and make the movement smoothly.

CONCLUSION

In this project, I've learned about how to create an animation from concept generation to dive into the software then adjust it frame by frame. It's a long process, but I really like the final result.
