Glacier: A contemporary display type face.

About the project

I am really excited to have a chance to create my own typeface. In this project, I decided to create an experimental font that can be used for display and headline.

Timeline

Oct. 2015 to Dec. 2015

Project Detail

Independent project

My Role

concept generation, type design, type specimen website

VISIT WEBSITE

CONTEXT

A glacier (US /ˈɡleɪʃər/ or UK /ˈɡlæsiə/) is a persistent body of dense ice that is constantly moving under its own weight; it forms where the accumulation of snow exceeds its ablation (melting and sublimation) over many years, often centuries.

The name of 'Glacier' was inspired by the movie Everest. After getting the inspiration and the research, I decided to create a series of the typeface with a hard edge and a mix of serif and sans-serif type.

PROCESS

Moodboard and Inspiration

I started to collect the typeface I loved and then created a moodboard. After looking for the inspiration, I found that a typeface with geometric shape and oblique lines are more suitable for the name of Glacier.

I then continued to experiment different structure of the glyphs.

Moodboard

Iteration

After creating the glyphs in Illustrator, I started to print them out in a bigger size. So that I could look closely at the type structure and fixed the problem. This iteration process took three times. Printed them out, found the problem then fixed it, until it looked perfect.

Print out the letters

Dive into the Font Lab

After I had the full version of all the glyphs, including upper and lower case of A-Z, punctuations, accents and numbers, I started to put them into the Font Lab. This is a very important step when creating a typeface. In this step, I adjusted the metrics and kerning of the words and letters back and forth to make sure that they would not have weird space between each other.
