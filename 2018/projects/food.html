Food For Kids: A compaign to against hunger

About the project

This project was inspired by a non-profit fundraising campaign, Red Nose Day, which raises money to keep children and young people safe, healthy and educated through different programs. Food For Kids is a campaign to help raise awareness of the American's hunger issue.

Timeline

Sep. 2015 to Oct. 2015

Project Detail

Independent project

My Role

Campaign, Branding

Back to Top

Children hunger is a serious problem

Target Audience

The Food For Kids campaign is targeting the kids from age 8 to 10, who are sympathetic and love to help people in need.

The Idea

I came up with several ideas about how to present the campaign, including posters, billboard ads, and interactive websites. And finally, I chose the interactive Kiosk and the Instagram Ads. The interactive kiosk in the shopping mall or department stores always attracts people's eyeball. The idea of the kiosk is to let kids learn more about the hunger problem in the U.S. and use an interesting way to spread the message around the world.

Interactive Kiosk

The interactive kiosk will put at the department store or other indoor space. The users can take a quiz to understand the hunger issue in the United States. Use the spoons beside the kiosk to create a spoon selfie and share some words to the world.

Kiosk is the trend to capture children’s interests.

As explained by Jeff Simpson, director of Deloitte Consulting LLD, “Retailers should focus on designing and building customer experiences that play to how their customers are shopping for their products.” For children, their willingness to experiment and use technology is a key consideration when merchandising; however, displays must have a broad appeal – attracting both children and their parents.

Source: Attracting a Tech-Savvy Generation through Education and Play
