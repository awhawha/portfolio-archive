Amazing Reef: An interactive app to learn about the ocean issue

About the project

Amazing Reef is an interactive app that teaches kids knowledge about coral reefs and how human activities can affect the ocean by taking journeys in different locations

Timeline

Sep. 2014 to May 2016

Project Detail

Personal Project, Thesis project

My Role

Concept, user experience, visual design and coding

THESIS BOOK

Context

The reason why I want to take coral reefs as my subject is because as we think about the endangered animals, our thought immediately sprints to Polar bears or tigers. But there are also a lot of creatures at risk, especially ocean creatures. Some of them disappear rapidly and we even don't know their names. When I visited the science museum, I found that the children were so excited to see the beautiful coral reefs, and they were also interested in learning about the ocean's threats. So I decided to combine the coral reefs and the environmental issue together to teach kids about how they can protect the ocean by their daily activities.

Overview

This application invites the kids from age 8 to 10 to take the journeys to learn about the risks that coral reefs are facing. The users can earn reward coral badges while answering secret ocean facts. Also, it engages kids with their parents to calculate the family carbon footprint and collects badges by taking real world action.

Project Goal

  • Help children learn about the ocean environmental issue via fun and interactive storyline
  • Encourage kids to make changes from their day to day activities to protect the ocean
  • Engage with parents to learn about the coral reefs and calculate the family carbon footprint

    • User Experience Process

    Background Research

    The challenge in the beginning of this project is to narrow down the scope and choose a focused area since marine conservation is a very broad topic. Because of my interest, I really what to know how people can make an impact on their daily activity to protect the environment.

    Topic and audience Research

    I visited the science museum and aquarium to observe visitors and met children and parents to get advice and inspirations to conceptualize the idea.

    Field Trip - Visit California Academy of Science

    Main findings:

  • Most of people have the awareness of the environmental issue, but it's hard to connect with their daily life
  • Almost all the kids were either very or a little bit worried about people damaging the planet. And protecting the ocean is an interesting field that can be futher develop.
  • Children from 8 to 10 are interested in the ocean creatures and also care about the environmental issue of the ocean.

    Card Sorting

    The card sorting testing helped me to know how user categorized the information and also help me to generate the Information Architecture of the app

    Survey

    I sent an inVision prototype and questionnaire via email to several experts or educators in the fields of coral reef science and conservation to test my idea and gather feedbacks from them.

    Below are the feedbacks from the testers.

    Overall I would include more descriptive illustrations and less text in all areas. Especially the long back and forth between the character and the diver in the beginning. - User

    Focusing on the meat industry and transportation industry is good. Also add energy show how coal is a major pollutant and alternatives like solar and wind energy. - User

    User Testing

    After collecting all the research results from usability testing, I have created a set of wireframes focusing on the first level of the game. The wireframes provide examples of before and after on all the screens in the first level of the game.

    The high fidelity wireframes included:

    • Give clear directions and instructions about how to navigate and interact with the app
    • Add headlines and hand gesture icons when needed in each page
    • Use character to connect the story together
